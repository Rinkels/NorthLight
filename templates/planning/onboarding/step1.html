Welcome to NorthLight

The objective is not to make every area of your life a 10.

NorthLight helps you answer a short sequence of questions, and return to them over time:

  1. Where am I now?
  2. What actually matters to me?
  3. In what direction do I want each part of my life to move — and why?
  4. What would meaningful improvement look like over the next year?
  5. What specific goals would demonstrate that?
  6. What should I do over the next 90 days?
  7. What should I protect rather than improve?

Setup takes a few minutes. Every step is skippable and everything is saved as you go.
